# Next-Stage Skill Review Protocol: Skill Master

This guide facilitates the review of your 180 backed-up skills one at a time using ChatGPT or Antigravity, ensuring no essential skill is deleted.

## Review Schema
For each skill listed in `INVENTORY.csv`:
1. **Skill Identifier & Path:** (e.g., `preference-frequency-manager` in `skills/workspace-ai-seo-geo/preference-frequency-manager`)
2. **Core Function:** Concise capability summary
3. **Trigger Contexts:** When and why this skill activates
4. **Primary Assets:** Included scripts, rules, or templates
5. **Sibling Overlap:** Overlap or synergy with other skills
6. **Goal Alignment:** Relevance to Web Dev, Frontend, UI/UX, Android, Architecture, Content, or Media
7. **Recommendation:** Keep, Investigate, or Consider Removing
8. **User Decision:** Final decision
